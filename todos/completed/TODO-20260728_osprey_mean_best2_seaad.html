Reproducibility aggregation in Osprey: what the sensitivity loss is, and what mean(best-N) recovers

SEA-AD Pilot-MTG Astral DIA, entrapment oracle (1:1 library decoys + entrapment). Every point is one Osprey first-pass arm; discoveries are counted at matched 1% TRUE FDP so arms are compared at equal honesty. Generated from arms across cohorts.

The loss. As runs accumulate the real proteome saturates while the null keeps accruing at a roughly constant rate, so each added file contributes fewer new real precursors and about as many new false ones. An experiment-wide max score accepts a precursor on the strength of one good run, so it inherits that collapsing purity and the 1% threshold must tighten — which costs identifications in every file.

The recovery. mean(best-N) demotes acceptances resting on a single run and spends the freed FDR headroom on reproducible precursors. It returns a minority of what scale costs.

The caveat that dominates everything. The size of that recovery is set by which files are in the cohort, not by how many. Five different 40-file cohorts range from +2.9% to +12.9%, and even at large scale the magnitude does not settle: three cohorts of 75–82 files give +6.7%, +10.3% and +14.6% — the two 75-file sets differ by 3.6 points despite sharing 91% of their files. Quote the range with a per-cohort uncertainty of roughly ±4 points; never a single cohort's number.

And it is not bought by loose FDR. Panel 2: every arm measured sits below the nominal 1%, and mean(best-2) is the more conservative member of the pair in most cohorts. The extra identifications come with better calibration, not worse.

Union efficiency = share of run-level detections that survive the experiment-wide cut. The union is computed from per-file passing, which the aggregation provably does not touch (verified byte-identical between arms), so cohorts of different size and quality are comparable.

2 · Calibration: is the sensitivity bought by under-reporting FDR?

True entrapment FDP measured at the reported q ≤ 1% cut. Below the dashed line is conservative (the reported q overstates the error); above it would mean the reported q is optimistic and any extra identifications are illusory. This is the panel that decides whether the recovery is real — a method that gained sensitivity by drifting above 1% would be worthless.

4 · The same files, sampled two ways

Matched-size pairs. Sparse sampling spans the whole acquisition series; contiguous takes a block. At 17 files sparse more than doubles the gain; at 28 files it halves it. The max baseline is what moves — and it moves with file choice, not file count.

5 · How deep should N go?

Aggregation depth swept within single cohorts. N=1 is max by definition (mean of the best 1 per-run score). Both large cohorts peak at N=3–6 rather than N=2, and the curve is forgiving above the peak but not below it.

6 · Mechanism

Left: purity of what the last ~10 files add to the accepted union — the marginal file contributes almost entirely false precursors once cohorts get large (the 1:1 estimator saturates at 100%). Right: where mean(best-2) moves acceptances, by the number of runs each acceptance rests on. Singletons always leave; the freed headroom goes to 2-run precursors in small cohorts and 6+-run precursors in large ones.
