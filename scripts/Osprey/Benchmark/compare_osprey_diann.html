Each tool scored against the other as ground truth

One tool's 1% IDs are the truth. The other tool's scored precursor-runs, best q first, count as found only when the precursor is a truth ID and the peaks match; every other call, including the right precursor on a different peak, counts as a false positive. The marker is the ranked tool's own 1% cut.
